What we collect

When you use a form on this site, we collect the details you type in: your name, phone number, email address (if you provide it), and your message. If you apply for a job, we also collect the role you chose and what you tell us about yourself. If you are a verified customer leaving a review, we collect your rating, your review, and the display name you choose.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to your request, provide your quote or service, consider your job application, or display your review on this site. Quote and application messages are delivered to the owner by text message. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Service providers

To run the site and deliver your message, we use trusted services for website hosting, secure message handling, and text message delivery. They process your information only as needed to provide those services.

How long we keep it

We keep a backup copy of quote requests and applications for up to 90 days, and then it is deleted automatically. Published reviews stay on the site until you ask us to remove them.

Reviews

Reviews can only be left by customers who received a private link from us after a job. Only the display name, rating, and review you submit are shown publicly. Your phone number and email are never shown.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your information, or to remove your review, any time by calling or texting 660-351-4699.
