About us

Home roots. Hard work. Real care.

The story behind Move Over KC, and the standards behind every job.

Hi, I'm Ryan.

I grew up in Lee's Summit, Missouri. After six years of moving around, including stops in Phoenix, Arizona and Denver, Colorado, I came back to my home roots. Lee's Summit is my home base, and I could not be prouder to build my business right here.

I know what a move feels like, because I have lived it more than a few times. It is stressful, it is physical, and you are trusting someone with the things that make your place feel like home. That trust is not something I take lightly.

Physical fitness has always been a big part of who I am, and so has genuinely helping people. I work in the life coaching space as well as managing and operating Move Over KC, and the thread connecting all of it is the same: show up, put in real effort, and make a real difference for the person in front of me.

I put a lot of passion and pride into every single job, and I treat every person's belongings with the utmost care and respect.
